## PhD positions

Open PhD positions are announced on the [Constructor University website]({{ site.institution.url }}) and in our [news](/news/). If no position is open, you can still get in touch to discuss funding opportunities such as scholarships and fellowships.

## Master's and Bachelor's projects

Students at Constructor University can do their thesis or a guided research project with us. Send an email with:

- a short paragraph about what interests you,
- your CV and transcript,
- any code or project you are proud of (a GitHub link is perfect).

## Visiting researchers

We welcome short research visits from students and researchers working on related topics.
